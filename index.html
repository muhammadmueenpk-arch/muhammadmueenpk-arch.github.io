About Me

From Physical Infrastructure
to Enterprise Networks

Enterprise networking isn't just about configuring routers and switches—it's about understanding the entire infrastructure that keeps businesses connected. That's the direction I'm building my career toward.

I'm currently expanding my expertise in Enterprise Networking, with a strong focus on Routing & Switching, TCP/IP, VLANs, Network Troubleshooting, and Network Security through my CCNA journey. My goal is to design, deploy, and maintain secure, reliable, and scalable enterprise networks that support modern organizations.

What makes my background different is that I don't only understand network configurations—I understand the infrastructure those networks run on.

With over 5+ years of hands-on experience across Saudi Arabia and Pakistan, I've worked on fiber optic installations, OTDR testing, GPON/FTTx deployments, structured cabling, rack installation, patch panel termination, and low-voltage systems. I've been part of projects where network reliability depended on getting the physical layer right from the very beginning.

That experience taught me an important lesson: many network issues don't start with configuration—they start with the physical infrastructure. A damaged fiber, poor termination, or incorrect installation can affect an entire network. Understanding both the physical and logical layers allows me to troubleshoot more effectively and build networks with long-term reliability in mind.

I believe the strongest network professionals are those who can bridge the gap between physical infrastructure and enterprise networking. That's the perspective I bring to every project—combining practical field experience with growing expertise in modern networking technologies.

Currently based in Jeddah, Saudi Arabia, I am actively seeking opportunities in Enterprise Networking, Network Support, Telecom Infrastructure, Data Centers, and BMS/ELV Systems. As I continue progressing toward CCNA, CCNP, and Network Security, my objective is to contribute to organizations by building secure, scalable, and high-performing network infrastructures that are designed to last.
